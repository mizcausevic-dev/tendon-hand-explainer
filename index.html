Mechanical model and keyboard

Eleven palm-mounted motor-capstan modules. Each of the four fingers has a flexor and an extensor. The thumb adds an opposition capstan. Distal joints are coupled to the joint before them. Abduction is a coupled spread bias with a passive web, not an independent motor.

A tendon pulls. Return motion comes from the opposing tendon. Capstan rotation, cable travel, and joint angle stay on the same clock. The taxel grid visualizes normal contact. Tangent marks are labeled “Illustrative shear estimate” and are not a claim that the grid measures shear.

Angles and pressure colors are illustrative animation data. They are not live sensing or a validated force simulation. Sound is synthesized for the film, not a recording of this mechanism.

Keys, when focus is not on a control: Space plays or pauses, Left and Right seek one second, Shift plus an arrow seeks a tenth of a second, Home and End jump to the ends, R replays, M mutes, F toggles fullscreen.
